## The Beginning

When I started my career in environmental science, I never imagined I'd end up as an Applied Scientist at Zalando. Life has a funny way of taking unexpected turns.

## The Transition

### PhD in Materials Science
During my PhD at TU Berlin, I focused on developing low-cost ceramic materials for water filtration. This research involved:

- Material synthesis and characterization
- Data analysis and statistical modeling
- Scientific writing and presentation
- **Patent development** (pending patent on fungal-based materials)

### Discovering Data Science
The turning point came when I realized that the analytical skills I developed in research were highly transferable to business problems.

### The Learning Path
I enrolled in the Practicum by Yandex Data Science bootcamp, which covered:

```python
# Skills learned
```

## Key Learnings

### Technical Skills Transfer
Many skills from academia transferred well:
- **Hypothesis testing** → A/B testing
- **Research methodology** → Experimental design
- **Data analysis** → Business analytics
- **Scientific writing** → Technical documentation

### New Challenges
Business environments brought new challenges:
- **Time constraints** vs. academic thoroughness
- **Stakeholder communication** vs. peer review
- **Business impact** vs. scientific contribution

## Current Role at Zalando
Now I work on performance marketing measurement and steering, focusing on:

1. **Attribution modeling** for campaign effectiveness
2. **Geo-experiments** for causal inference
3. **MMM (Mixed Media Modeling)** for holistic measurement
4. **Customer lifetime value** prediction

## Advice for Career Changers

### Do
- ✅ Leverage your existing analytical skills
- ✅ Build a portfolio of projects
- ✅ Network with data science professionals
- ✅ Start with online courses and bootcamps

### Don't
- ❌ Expect immediate success
- ❌ Underestimate the business side
- ❌ Ignore soft skills development
- ❌ Stop learning after landing your first role

## Conclusion

Career transitions are challenging but rewarding. The combination of domain expertise and data science skills can be a powerful differentiator.
